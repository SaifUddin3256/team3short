Who We Are

About Team 3Short

Team 3Short is a fully online-managed PUBG Mobile creator team based in Bangladesh. We bring together passionate content creators across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok — growing our audiences and building opportunities together instead of alone. With no physical gaming house, every member creates on their own channel while representing Team 3Short and staying connected through our active Discord community.

Our Vision

Where We're Headed

Our vision is to build Bangladesh's most trusted and organized PUBG Mobile creator community — a place where creators of any size can grow together, gain real opportunities, and represent something bigger than themselves. We aim to set the standard for professionalism and teamwork in mobile esports content creation.
